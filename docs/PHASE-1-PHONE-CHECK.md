# Phase 1: 5-minute phone check

Open the app in **Expo Go** on your iPhone, then go down the list. Anything that looks off, screenshot it and send it to me.

- [ ] The welcome screen shows the tilted pink **I'M IN** logo and three sticker badges.
- [ ] **Create Account:** try a birth year that makes you 16. It should say you must be 18.
- [ ] Sign up for real. The keyboard shouldn't cover the field you're typing in.
- [ ] Add a profile photo during signup. It should show on your profile afterward.
- [ ] After signup, the **Your First Week** popup appears. Tap **Got it**.
- [ ] The tab bar shows **Home, Pins, Tonight, Circles**, and nothing sits under the iPhone home bar.
- [ ] Tap your avatar (top right). Your profile opens with a back arrow.
- [ ] Menu → **Appearance** → try all five themes. The whole app changes each time.
- [ ] Make text huge: iPhone **Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text**. Come back: text should be bigger, nothing cut off.
- [ ] Log out (Menu → Sign Out), then log back in **with your phone number** instead of your email.
- [ ] Forgot password: you should get the email (check spam).
