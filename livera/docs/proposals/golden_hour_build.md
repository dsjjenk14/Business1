# Golden Hour, with the best of Talk of the Town (Release 5, the owner's combination)

The owner, after D-071: "I like Golden Hour more now; combine the two as best as possible." So Release 5 is built on
Studio Kinetic's **Golden Hour** (`showdown/golden_hour.md`: "Days are yours. Nights are ours.") and takes from
Studio Ember's **Talk of the Town** (`showdown/talk_of_the_town.md`) everything that makes it better. This replaces
`talk_of_the_town_build.md` as the plan; the decision is D-076.

What the owner already said about both pitches still holds, so where Golden Hour cuts something the owner kept, the
owner wins:

- **Chores stay** on screen and playable (Golden Hour took them off the screen).
- **The day isn't shrunk**: it is still lived hour by hour on the open map. Golden Hour's day planner is an *option*
  for a busy day, never the only way to play one.
- **Social media, not a morning paper**: Golden Hour's Morning Ledger becomes the social app's morning, built from
  what really happened.
- **More open world**: you can walk anywhere at any hour; Golden Hour's "travel happens in the Day" is not taken.
- The faster clock (D-075), money, a useful phone, better sitting and more people.

## The frame, from Golden Hour

**Days are yours, nights are ours.** The day is yours to live as you like: work, chores, errands, a date, a scheme,
walking the town. Every evening the light turns gold and the town goes live: venues fill, your friends come online,
and the night is where the stories happen.

1. **Golden Hour every evening.** A window in the data (start and end hour). The town is lit by the clock (champagne
   by day, peach from late afternoon, an emerald-navy night with lit windows and lamps, brightest at the busiest
   venues). Founders and townspeople head out for the evening (their schedules), so the Copper Tap, Lumière, Low End
   Studios and Heritage Coffee fill up. In Play together it is the crew's live time: the faces row in a room shows
   who is live (green) and who is on autopilot (grey, labelled).
2. **Beat Brawl, sharpened by what you know.** Golden Hour's fight: three rounds of beats, about a second each and
   faster every round; the other fighter's tell (HOOK, JAB, SHOVE) shows over their head and you answer Duck, Block
   or Jab before the ring closes; a slip or a parry leaves them open for a counter; perfect timing hits harder. The
   edge comes from the core's own score formula (athletics, energy, health, injuries), so sleep and the gym matter at
   night. From Talk of the Town: **knowledge sharpens tells**. If someone told you how Andre fights, his tells show
   earlier and never lie. Every D-070 rule stays: the person attacked chooses, "always walk away", assault only when
   a blow lands, self-defence is never a crime, one fight a pair a day, the cooldown and protection, and the odds
   and the ladder first. The World judges each beat; the client adds the feel (hit-stop, shake, flash, sparks,
   sound, vibration; all off with reduce motion).
3. **Group moments.** A life-event card that lands on everyone in the room at once; each person picks in secret, the
   faces flip to show the picks, and the outcome comes from the combination ("One more round": a bidding war, or the
   best person in the bar for a minute; the bill at Lumière: if everyone goes to the restroom, it's a dine-and-dash).
4. **The room camera.** A close camera that follows you in a room, pathfinding around the traced furniture, furniture
   drawn over the people behind it, one context button (Sit down, Talk to Dax, Stand up), drag and pinch.
5. **Heart, Hustle and Heat.** Three reputations anyone can read in *Your life*: Heart (friends, love, family),
   Hustle (work, money, homes), Heat (the street). Under each, Talk of the Town's witnessed tags (Cool Head, Hothead,
   Keeps Secrets, Big Mouth, Generous, Fighter) say why.
6. **The crew.** Callouts are friends asking ("Rematch, Friday", "Copper Tap at 9?"), accepted or declined by the
   World's rules; the crew chat; at most one notification a day from the game, everything else from a friend.
7. **An optional day planner.** For a busy day: pick Morning, Afternoon and Tonight from two or three choices each,
   tap *Live my day*, and your routine (the autopilot the founders use) lives it at speed, chores included, stopping
   for cards and handing back the moment you tap. Or don't, and play it hour by hour.
8. **Money.** The **Golden Hour Pass**, a cosmetic season (outfits for the night, decor, emotes, entrance and victory
   poses), and the **Town Pass**, one person pays for the crew's always-on town. Never power, never a way round
   consent. Nothing is charged until the owner picks a payment provider.

## The best of Talk of the Town

- **Social media is how word gets around** (owner's choice over the paper): the morning's "Last night in Livéra" in
  the social app is Golden Hour's Ledger as a feed: the top posts of the night, @livera.unfiltered's, rumours with
  "heard by N" and whether they're true, and tonight's callouts. Whoever plays Sienna secretly runs Unfiltered.
- **Scenes and secrets:** moves on a person in a room (warm, bold, secret, rough); who knows what; secrets open new
  moves and sharpen fight tells.
- **Story threads** from the founders' real secrets (Miami, the Maple Court flip, the Parallax demo, who runs
  Unfiltered).
- **Standing orders** for when you're away (fight back, walk away, avoid trouble); anything big waits for you; "While
  you were away" tells you what happened.
- **The sprite-sheet frames** (Ember's cutter), whole bodies walking and sitting: already being built.
- **The scene director:** evening plans from schedules, traits and threads so venues fill at golden hour.

## Not taken, and why

| From | Not taken | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Golden Hour | Chores off the screen, the montage-only day, travel only in the Day | The owner kept chores, the full day and the open map |
| Golden Hour | The Morning Ledger newspaper | The owner chose social media; its content moves to the feed |
| Talk of the Town | Four beats and "scene time" stopping the clock | The full day stays; the faster clock and a speed control make it fit |
| Talk of the Town | Square Up | Beat Brawl is the fight; Square Up's best idea (knowledge sharpens tells) is in it |
| Talk of the Town | A mood ring instead of the needs | Chores are back, so the needs that drive them stay visible |
| Both | A turn clock for shared towns | Later, with the always-on server (the Town Pass) |
