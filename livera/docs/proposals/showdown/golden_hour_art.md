# LIVÉRA: Golden Hour, the art we're asking for

Studio Kinetic's redesign needs ten new pictures. They're written like `art/PROMPT_PACK_CHARACTERS.md` and `art/PROMPT_PACK_INTERIORS.md`, so each one can be pasted on its own. Every prompt repeats the style in brief. The people's descriptions are copied word for word from the character pack, which builds them from `data/characters`.

**Order of importance.** 1–5 first (the fight sheets: Beat Brawl looks stiff without them), then 7 (the alley, where most fights should happen), then the rest.

**How to use.** Same as the packs: copy one prompt, attach the reference sheet it names, check that it's the same person in every cell, at the town's angle and the height the prompt gives, with nothing cropped and no background. Save it under the file name at the end of the prompt.

## The style, in brief

- **View:** the town's camera, isometric 2:1 (30°) from the south-west, looking down at about 30°, the same as the sprite sheets and the interiors.
- **Light:** golden hour from the west, a soft champagne glow and a warm rim light. The game adds night itself.
- **People:** one scale for everyone: a 175 cm person is 300 px tall in a 341 px cell. Full body, nothing cropped, feet on one baseline per row. Transparent background, no ground shadow.
- **Fights:** fists only, no weapons, no blood, no gore. It's a scuffle in a nice bar, not a beating. Clothes stay on and intact. Every pose reads clearly at 100 px tall.
- **No brands, no text.**
