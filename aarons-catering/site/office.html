How it works

Made for offices

  • Per-person pricing

    One price per person, so the total is easy to budget. Delivery and tax are added on the quote.

  • Order ahead

    Two business days’ notice for most orders, a week for the chef-attended breakfast.

  • Delivered and set up

    We arrive ahead of time, set up, and bring plates, napkins and serving utensils.

  • Allergens listed

    Every dish below lists its allergens. Tell us about allergies when you order.

  • Standing orders

    A weekly team lunch or a monthly breakfast, on the same day each time.

Allergies. This is a working kitchen that handles shellfish, dairy, eggs, wheat and nuts. We take real care, but we cannot guarantee any dish is free of an allergen. Every dish lists its allergens; if anyone on your team has a food allergy, tell us when you order or call 571-271-1810.
