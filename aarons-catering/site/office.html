Entrées

  • Baked or grilled chicken

    Bone-in chicken seasoned with garlic, lemon, rosemary and paprika, baked or grilled.

    Allergens: None in the recipe. See the allergy note.

    Ingredients

    Bone-in chicken thighs and breasts, olive oil, garlic, lemon, fresh rosemary, fresh thyme, paprika, onion powder, kosher salt, black pepper.

  • Southern fried chicken

    Bone-in chicken marinated in buttermilk and hot sauce, seasoned and fried crisp.

    Allergens: Dairy, Wheat

    Ingredients

    Chicken thighs and drumsticks, buttermilk, hot sauce (aged cayenne peppers, distilled vinegar, salt, garlic), all-purpose wheat flour, cornstarch, kosher salt, black pepper, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne pepper, canola oil for frying.

  • Lemon herbed salmon

    Salmon fillets roasted with lemon, garlic, dill and parsley, finished with a little butter.

    Allergens: Fish, Dairy

    Ingredients

    Atlantic salmon, lemon, garlic, fresh dill, parsley, fresh thyme, olive oil, butter, kosher salt, black pepper.

  • Smothered turkey wings

    Turkey wings baked until tender and smothered in onion and sage gravy.

    Allergens: Dairy, Wheat

    Ingredients

    Turkey wings, yellow onion, celery, garlic, all-purpose wheat flour, chicken stock (chicken bones, water, onion, carrot, celery), butter, fresh sage, fresh thyme, smoked paprika, kosher salt, black pepper.

  • Fried fish

    Whiting or catfish in a seasoned cornmeal crust, fried golden. Lemon and tartar sauce on the side.

    Allergens: Fish, Eggs, Wheat, Mustard

    Ingredients

    Whiting or catfish fillets, yellow mustard, hot sauce (aged cayenne peppers, distilled vinegar, salt, garlic), yellow cornmeal, all-purpose wheat flour, Cajun seasoning (paprika, garlic, onion, cayenne, oregano, thyme, black pepper, salt), canola oil for frying, lemon, tartar sauce (mayonnaise (canola oil, egg yolks, distilled vinegar, lemon juice, salt), dill relish, lemon juice, onion, parsley).
