From the kitchen

What people ask for

All menus

  • Chicken and waffles

    Chicken brined overnight in buttermilk, fried crisp and set next to buttermilk waffles with warm syrup and fresh strawberries.

  • Braised short ribs

    Seared dark, then braised four hours with onion, carrot, garlic and thyme until a fork goes straight through.

  • Five-cheese baked macaroni

    Five cheeses set in an egg custard and baked in the pan we serve it in. Ask for a corner piece.

  • Cajun crab and shrimp

    Crab clusters, shrimp and andouille in a Cajun garlic butter sauce with peppers and onion. Napkins on the side.

The chef

Chef Aaron Jenkins

Soul food takes time. Greens need three hours. Short ribs need four. Chicken needs a night in the brine. I don’t cut those corners, and I taste every pan before it leaves the kitchen.

Everything we serve is cooked from scratch in our kitchen in Fairfax, the day before or the morning of your event.
