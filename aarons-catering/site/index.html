Signatures

Before you book

Questions, answered

Anything else? Call 571-271-1810.

How far ahead should we book?

For weddings and large events, as soon as you have a date, since weekends go first. Two business days’ notice for most orders, a week for the chef-attended breakfast. Thanksgiving orders close November 20 and Christmas orders close December 18, or sooner if we sell out.

Where do you cater?

Washington, DC, Maryland and Northern Virginia. Delivery is priced on your quote, and holiday delivery fees are listed on the Holiday page.

Can you work around allergies and diets?

Every dish on our menus lists its ingredients and allergens, and vegetarian and vegan dishes are marked. Ours is a working kitchen that handles shellfish, dairy, eggs, wheat and nuts, so we can’t promise any dish is free of an allergen. Tell us about allergies when you order.

Is there a minimum?

Office packages start at 10 people (20 for the chef-attended breakfast). Holiday packages start with Dinner for Two. For events, tell us your headcount and we’ll suggest the right service style.

Do you bring staff and set up?

Office orders arrive ahead of time and are set out with plates, napkins and serving utensils. For events and weddings, your quote covers the service you choose, from drop-off to a plated dinner with servers.

How do we pay?

Holiday orders are paid by card when you order online. For events and office catering, payment details come with your quote.
