Catering menus

Soul food catering menus

Starting points, not rules. Mix across menus, swap a side, add a protein. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll price it for your guest count.

Allergies

Our kitchen handles shellfish, dairy, eggs, wheat and nuts. Everything is prepared in the same space, so we cannot guarantee that any dish is free of allergens, even when the recipe doesn’t include them.

Every dish below lists the allergens in its recipe. If someone at your event has a serious allergy, call us at 571-271-1810 before you order so we can talk it through.

Pricing depends on guest count, service style and location. Request a quote and we’ll send a full breakdown.

Weddings

Buffet, family-style or plated. Most couples choose two mains, three sides, bread and one or two desserts. We cook a tasting for the two of you about six weeks out, so nothing on the day is a surprise.

Passed bites

  • Shrimp and grits cups

    Stone-ground grits with white cheddar and butter, topped with a seared shrimp and a little smoked paprika butter. Two bites.

    Ingredients Shrimp, stone-ground white grits, white cheddar, butter, heavy cream, chicken stock (chicken bones, water, onion, carrot, celery), garlic, smoked paprika, Cajun seasoning (paprika, garlic, onion, cayenne, oregano, thyme, black pepper, salt), lemon, scallions.

    Allergens Shellfish, Dairy

  • Handmade mini quiche

    Two-bite quiches in an all-butter crust. Spinach and cheddar, or ham and Swiss.

    Ingredients All-butter crust (all-purpose wheat flour, butter, ice water, sugar, salt), eggs, heavy cream, whole milk, spinach, cheddar, Swiss cheese, ham (pork, water, salt, sugar, sodium nitrite), yellow onion, nutmeg, kosher salt, black pepper.

    Allergens Dairy, Eggs, Wheat

  • Fried green tomatoes

    Green tomatoes cut thick, dipped in buttermilk and egg, crusted in cornmeal and fried. Topped with comeback sauce.
