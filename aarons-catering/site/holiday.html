How to order

Four steps

  1. 01

    Pick a package

    Or build your own from the list above. Note your sides and desserts.

  2. 02

    Send us your order

    Use the order form or call 571-271-1810. Tell us pickup or delivery, and which day.

  3. 03

    We confirm it

    We reply within one business day with your total and an invoice. Your order is held once the invoice is paid.

  4. 04

    Pick up or get delivery

    Everything comes fully cooked and chilled, with reheating instructions on every lid.

Pickup

From our kitchen in Fairfax, during the pickup window for your holiday. We send the address and your time with your confirmation. Pans travel best flat, so bring a box or clear the back seat.

Delivery

$40 within 20 miles of Fairfax. Further into DC or Maryland, we quote it when you order. Someone needs to be there to take it in.

Reheating

Most of the meal reheats covered at 325°F in 30 to 45 minutes. A whole turkey takes about 90. Instructions are taped to every lid.
