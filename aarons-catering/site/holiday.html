How to order

Four steps

  1. 01

    Pick your package

    Choose your holiday, then your meats, sides and dessert. Jerk turkey, short ribs, crab-stuffed shrimp and seafood salad must be requested by the order deadline.

  2. 02

    Order online

    Order on this site or call 571-271-1810. Thanksgiving orders close Friday, November 20 and Christmas orders close Friday, December 18, or sooner if we sell out.

  3. 03

    Pay to confirm

    Payment in full is due when you order. Your order isn’t confirmed until it’s paid. Cancel by the order deadline for a full refund. After that the food is already bought and prepped, so orders are final.

  4. 04

    Pickup or delivery

    Thanksgiving goes out Tuesday, November 24 to Wednesday, November 25: delivery until 1 pm Wednesday, pickup until 3 pm Wednesday. Christmas goes out Thursday, December 24: delivery by 11 am, pickup by 1 pm.

Food allergies

Tell us before you order. If anyone at your table has a food allergy, let us know when you place the order so we can talk it through with you honestly before you pay.

Seafood salad and crab-stuffed shrimp are prepared separately from the rest of the menu.

This is a working kitchen that handles shellfish, dairy, eggs, wheat and nuts. We take real care, but we cannot guarantee any dish is free of an allergen and we do not make that promise. If an allergy is severe, please make your own call.
