How to order

Four steps

  1. 01

    Pick your package

    Choose your meats, sides and dessert from the menu. Jerk turkey, short ribs, crab-stuffed shrimp and seafood salad must be requested by Friday, November 20.

  2. 02

    Send us your order

    Use the order form or call 571-271-1810. Orders close Friday, November 20, or sooner if we sell out.

  3. 03

    Pay to confirm

    Payment in full is due when you order. Your order isn’t confirmed until it’s paid. Cancel by November 20 for a full refund. After that the food is already bought and prepped, so orders are final.

  4. 04

    Pickup or delivery

    Everything goes out Wednesday, November 25, the day before Thanksgiving. You get a 30-minute window when you order.

Tell us before you order. If anyone at your table has a food allergy, let us know when you place the order so we can talk it through with you honestly before you pay.
